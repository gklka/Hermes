Hermes is a free, open-source Facebook Messenger client written in Swift and AppKit. No Electron, no web page in a window: it looks and feels like Apple's Messages.

Built like Messages

Everything you chat with,
the way a Mac does it.

Replies, tapbacks, polls, themes and calls, drawn with Apple's own controls. Every screenshot here is the real app, running on made-up chats.

Tapbacks, replies and read pictures.

Double-click a message to react, just like in Messages. Replies show their quote with a line to the message they answer.

  • Tapbacks and every quick reaction Messenger has
  • Pictures of who has read up to where
  • The typing bubble turns into the message when it arrives
  • Link previews, photos with Quick Look, drag and drop

Polls, themes and @mentions.

Group polls look like they do in Messages: a bubble per choice that grows with its votes and shows who voted. Click to vote, or add a choice.

  • Each chat's Messenger theme, gradients included
  • Tag people with @ in group chats
  • Light and dark mode

Audio and video calls.

Calls ring with a banner in the corner of your screen, like a system notification. Missed and finished calls show up in the chat as cards with Call Back.

  • Start a call from the toolbar
  • Shared locations as maps
  • Shared posts, reels and stories as rich cards

Chats in their own windows.

Double-click any chat to open it in a window of its own, with its picture, name and buttons in the title bar. The toolbars are customizable, in the main window and in chat windows.

  • Full menus and keyboard shortcuts
  • Three-finger swipes between chats
  • Chats open instantly: messages are stored on your Mac

How it works.

A Swift app on top of a Go core, built on the open-source libraries behind the mautrix Messenger bridge.

Native UI

Swift and AppKit, with the same controls, sheets, menus and Liquid Glass as Apple's own apps.

Messenger's protocol

A Go core speaks Messenger's web protocol through mautrix-meta, and handles encrypted chats with whatsmeow.

Your data stays on your Mac

Messages, keys and your session are stored locally and go only to Meta's servers. No analytics, no other servers.
